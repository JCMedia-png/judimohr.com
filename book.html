A heads up before we talk

You’ve probably noticed this in your own life: the people who get the most out of any kind of help are the ones who show up ready to be honest about where things stand, even when it feels messy. Those are the women I do my best work with.

If you’d rather keep things hidden, or you’re only looking for a hot tip, I’m probably not the right fit, and that’s okay. But if you’re ready to see the whole picture, I can’t wait to meet you.

One thing that will help you get the most from our time

If it’s easy, make a simple list of the accounts you know about: retirement accounts, savings, pensions, insurance policies. It doesn’t need to be complete or organized. Finding everything can be the first thing we do together.

If someone else is involved in your money decisions, a spouse, a partner or an adult child, you’re welcome to bring them. It saves you time later passing everything along.

The most common questions

What happens in the complimentary conversation?

We’ll sit down, talk through where you are and where you want to go, and you’ll leave with a clear picture of where you stand. No pressure, no jargon, just clarity.

Do I need to have everything organized first?

No. You do not need to solve your whole financial life today. Start with the question that is creating the most uncertainty.

What if I don’t know the right questions to ask?

You’re not bad with money. Nobody ever took the time to explain it to you in a way that made sense. That’s what I’m here for.
